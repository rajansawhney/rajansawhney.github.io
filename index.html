I am currently a computational biology Ph.D. student at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa, and I am interested in applied machine learning for biology.

My focal areas of research are on deep learning methods for cellular and molecular interactions, protein design for CRISPR-based therapeutics, virology, and immunology while aiming for equitable impact. My publications are available on Google Scholar.

Before pursuing my PhD with Dr. Mahdi Belcaid at the SAIL laboratory, I worked as Data Scientist and Software Engineer at global companies such as Intel and Accenture for several years.

I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Oregon under supervision of Dr. Boyanna Norris and Dr. Steven Fickas, with a concentration in machine learning and high-performance computing.
